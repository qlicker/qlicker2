# Getting Started with Qlicker

This guide covers tasks shared by students, professors, teaching assistants, and administrators. Your institution may enable only SSO, only email/password login, or both.

## Sign in or create an account

1. Open the Qlicker address supplied by your institution.
2. Choose the institution sign-in button when your school uses SSO. Otherwise, enter your email and password.
3. If self-registration is available, create an account with the email address your institution expects.
4. Complete email verification if the site requires it.

If your account was created by SSO, local name/password fields and password-reset email may be disabled. That is intentional unless an administrator grants your account permission to use local email login.

## Find your way around

The Qlicker logo or **Dashboard** menu item returns to the dashboard for your current role. The avatar menu in the upper-right contains:

- dashboard and role-appropriate course shortcuts
- notifications
- the in-app user manual
- profile settings
- logout

People with more than one role may see additional destinations. A student who is assigned as an instructor in a course can use the professor view for that course even if their global account role remains student.

## Read and dismiss notifications

The badge on your avatar indicates active notices. Open **Notifications** to see system-wide or course-specific announcements. Dismissing a notice removes it from your own view; it does not delete the notice for everyone else.

This is a read-only inbox. To write a notice, admins use **Users → Manage notifications** ([instructions](admin.md#send-notifications)); course instructors use **Students** or **Instructors → Manage notifications** ([instructions](professor.md#course-notices)).

## Update your profile

Open **Profile** from the avatar menu to change the settings your account permits:

- preferred interface language
- first and last name for local accounts
- password for accounts allowed to use local login
- profile image

Uploading an image opens a crop-and-rotate editor. Position the square crop, rotate if necessary, preview the avatar, and save. Use an image you are comfortable showing to instructors and classmates wherever avatars appear.

## Browser and accessibility tips

- Use a current version of Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari.
- Allow cookies and JavaScript for the Qlicker site; authentication and live updates depend on them.
- Keep only one quiz window open unless an instructor tells you otherwise.
- Qlicker supports keyboard navigation and responsive layouts. At narrow widths, long tab sets may appear in a **View** selector.
- If a live screen stops updating, confirm the network connection, then reload once. Saved quiz answers are restored from the server when available.

## Protect your account

- Do not share passwords, quiz links, passcodes, or live join codes outside the intended class.
- Log out on shared devices.
- Never paste SSO certificates, API tokens, or storage credentials into course content or support messages.
- Contact the site administrator if an unexpected role, course, or account restriction appears.
